DOORS

A GAME ABOUT EXIT WEST BY MOHSIN HAMID

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE: ARROW KEYS / WASD. RUN: HOLD SHIFT (USES STAMINA). TALK / USE: E WHEN YOU SEE THE PROMPT. CONTINUE: ENTER. CHOOSE: 1 / 2 / 3 OR CLICK. SOUND: M. RESTART: R.

HOPE: THE HEARTS. PATROLS TAKE ONE WHEN THEY CATCH YOU. HELPING PEOPLE AND RESTING GIVES ONE BACK. IF HOPE RUNS OUT, YOU TURN BACK.

GOAL: EACH PLACE HAS AN OBJECTIVE. FINISH IT, THEN WALK INTO THE GLOWING DOOR. THE DOOR REMOVES THE JOURNEY — NOT THE CONSEQUENCES.

WHAT IT IS ABOUT: IN EXIT WEST, DOORS LET PEOPLE STEP INSTANTLY FROM ONE COUNTRY TO ANOTHER. THE GAME ARGUES THAT ARRIVAL IS INSTANT, BUT BELONGING, SAFETY, AND WHAT MIGRATION COSTS THE PEOPLE YOU LOVE ARE NOT.
