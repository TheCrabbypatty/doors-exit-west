DOORS

A GAME ABOUT EXIT WEST BY MOHSIN HAMID

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE: ARROW KEYS / WASD. RUN: HOLD SHIFT (USES STAMINA). TALK / USE: E WHEN YOU SEE THE PROMPT. CONTINUE: ENTER. CHOOSE: 1 / 2 / 3 OR CLICK. JOURNAL: J TO READ THE MEMORIES YOU COLLECTED. SOUND: M. RESTART: R. THERE IS A HIDDEN STAR IN EVERY PLACE. WALK OVER IT TO COLLECT IT.

HOPE: THE HEARTS. PATROLS TAKE ONE WHEN THEY CATCH YOU. HELPING PEOPLE AND RESTING GIVES ONE BACK. IF HOPE RUNS OUT, YOU TURN BACK.

MEMORIES: THE GLOWING PAGES. EACH ONE IS A MOMENT FROM THE BOOK. COLLECT THEM.

GOAL: EACH PLACE HAS A LIST OF THINGS TO DO. FINISH THEM, THEN WALK INTO THE GLOWING DOOR.
